How to apply

DIRECTIONS FOR USE

Follow the section that matches how you plant. These are the labeled instructions for Nellie's Garden frass.

Potted Plants & Containers Vegetable Gardens & Flower Beds Transplants FOR BEST RESULTS STORAGE & HANDLING

Potted Plants & Containers

For new plantings, thoroughly mix 1 tablespoon per gallon of potting soil before planting. For established plants, gently work 1 tablespoon per gallon of container soil into the surface, avoiding roots and stems. Water thoroughly. Repeat every 4 weeks during active growth.

Vegetable Gardens & Flower Beds

Before planting, evenly distribute 1 pound per 100 square feet and mix into the top 3 inches of soil. Water thoroughly.

Transplants

Mix 1 tablespoon thoroughly into the soil surrounding each planting hole. Avoid placing concentrated frass directly against roots. Plant and water well.

FOR BEST RESULTS

Distribute evenly and always water after application. Use soil testing to guide additional fertilizer applications. Do not use frass alone as a potting medium.

STORAGE & HANDLING

Reseal after use. Store in a cool, dry place. Avoid breathing dust and wash hands after handling. Keep out of reach of children and pets. Not for human or animal consumption.
